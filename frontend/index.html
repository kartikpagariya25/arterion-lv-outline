What you get

A binary mask, a tinted overlay, and a simplified outline polygon (normalised 0–1) from the /lv-outline/predict endpoint — ready to feed a volume or EF calculation.

How it was trained

ResNet-34 U-Net, Dice + focal-Tversky loss, on masks built from the EchoNet-Dynamic expert tracings. Input is resized to 112×112; only the largest connected region is kept.

Limits

Trained on apical four-chamber views from a single centre. Other views, poor windows and out-of-distribution scanners may fail or trace the wrong cavity.
