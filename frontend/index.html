What you get

A binary mask, a tinted overlay and a simplified outline polygon, normalised from 0 to 1, from the /lv-outline/predict endpoint. It is ready to feed a volume or EF calculation.

How it was trained

ResNet-34 U-Net with Dice and focal-Tversky loss, on masks built from the EchoNet-Dynamic expert tracings. Input is resized to 112 by 112, and only the largest connected region is kept.

Limits

Trained on apical four-chamber views from a single centre. Other views, poor windows and other scanners may fail or trace the wrong cavity.
